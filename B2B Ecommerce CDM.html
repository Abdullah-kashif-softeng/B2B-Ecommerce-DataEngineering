The business concepts in the b2b_ecommerce catalog and how they relate. No tables, columns, keys or counts.

What to know before relying on it

  • The catalog declares almost no key constraints, so the links between concepts are inferred from matching identifier columns.
  • A Web Session is tied to its Page Requests only by shared visitor details, so that link is the least certain.
