Checked against the data

  • Every primary key and alternate key shown is unique across all rows, and no foreign key points at a missing row.
  • Every order has at least one item, and every supplier maps to its own company, which is why those ends read "one or many" and "zero or one".
  • A Web Session has a Customer for 29,630 of 110,848 sessions, and a Device is missing for 1,690 sessions and 9,698 page requests. Those links are drawn as optional.
  • A Page Request names its Customer through auth_user, which holds the customer's email. 214,131 of 538,152 requests have one, every one of them matches a Customer, and email is unique. The other 324,021 requests are anonymous.
  • Lead has no company key. It is tied to a Company only by matching company_name to the Company name, ignoring case. 144 of 1,098 leads match, so that line is dashed and zero or one.
  • An IP Address has no matching IP Geo Reference for 4,274 addresses and no first octet for 1,355, so that link is optional.
  • cuit is shared by some companies (356 distinct values across 425), so it is not a key.

Choices in this version

  • Web Sessions and Page Requests have no key between them, so that line is dashed. The catalog builds each session from page requests with the same IP address and device, split after 30 minutes of inactivity.
  • Lead repeats some company details (name, industry, country, city) because a lead can exist before any Company record. Order Item line_total and Web Session duration_seconds are shown as stored, though both could be calculated.
  • Left out because they are storage detail that belongs in the physical model: surrogate keys, created_at and updated_at timestamps, source_file and version validity columns. The gold PDM leaves the timestamps out too, by the catalog's own policy.
  • Attribute names are the catalog's own. Rename them to business terms once the definitions are agreed.
