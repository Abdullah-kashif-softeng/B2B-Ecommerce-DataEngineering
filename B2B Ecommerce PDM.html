What the catalog declares and what is inferred

  • The only declared constraint is pk_ref_ip_geo on ref_ip_geo.first_octet. Every other key and join is inferred from column names and checked against the data.
  • Each logical key is unique across all rows (for the bridge, supplier_id and product_id together), and no join has an orphan row. The exception is dim_ip_geo.first_octet: 4,274 addresses carry an octet that is not in the 21-row reference, and 1,355 have none.
  • Where Databricks does accept primary and foreign key constraints, it records them as metadata and does not check writes against them.
  • Facts carry client_ip and user_agent as text and join to dim_ip_geo and dim_device on those, so ip_sk and device_sk are not used by any join.

How it is stored

  • All 13 tables are Delta. The 12 built by the pipeline are streaming tables with zstd compression, deletion vectors, row tracking and change data feed turned on. ref_ip_geo is a plain managed table.
  • Table types and the slowly changing dimension type (SCD1 or SCD2) come from the table comments in the catalog.
